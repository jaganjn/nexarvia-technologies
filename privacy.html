PRIVACY NOTICE

How Nexarvia handles submitted information.

Effective: 5 August 2026. This notice covers Nexarvia Learning applications, live application progress and Nexarvia Technology Services business enquiries.

Information collected

Nexarvia Learning may collect contact information, academic or professional status, selected career domain, consent choices, referral details and temporary form-progress information. Nexarvia Technology Services may collect contact information, organisation, location, requested service, budget range, timeline, project requirements and contact preferences.

How information is used

Information is used to review applications or enquiries, communicate relevant next steps, provide authorised support, maintain internal records, measure form performance and improve the digital experience.

Separate data records

Learning applications and Technology Services enquiries are stored in separate Firebase database paths and viewed through authenticated administration. They should not be combined for unrelated purposes.

Live application progress

While a learner completes the application, entered fields, current step, completion percentage and activity time may be temporarily synchronised for authorised application monitoring and follow-up. A local browser draft may also be stored for a limited period.

Service providers

Firebase and authorised Google workflows may be used to store or process information. Nexarvia should apply reasonable access controls and should not sell personal information for unrelated advertising.

Choices and consent

Required consent confirms the applicable privacy and programme or service-enquiry terms. Optional communication consent can be withdrawn through an official Nexarvia contact channel.

Security and retention

Information should be retained only for legitimate application, enquiry, programme, delivery, compliance and support needs, then deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

Do not submit sensitive credentials

Do not submit passwords, banking PINs, government identity numbers, payment-card information, private cryptographic keys or other highly sensitive credentials through public forms.
