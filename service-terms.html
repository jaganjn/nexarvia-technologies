SERVICE ENQUIRY TERMS

Important conditions for Technology Services enquiries.

Effective: 5 August 2026. These terms describe the initial enquiry stage and do not replace a signed proposal, statement of work or service agreement.

1. Enquiry status

Submitting an enquiry records your interest and requirement. It does not automatically confirm project acceptance, pricing, delivery dates, resources or service availability.

2. Information provided

You should provide accurate, authorised and non-confidential information appropriate for an initial review. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, banking PINs, private cryptographic keys or information you are not authorised to share.

3. Scope and quotation

Any scope, schedule, fee, payment terms, responsibilities, support conditions and acceptance criteria will be confirmed separately in writing before work begins.

4. Intellectual property and third-party services

Ownership, licensing, third-party platforms, hosting, domains, paid APIs, software subscriptions and content responsibilities will be defined in the applicable project agreement.

5. Communication

Nexarvia may use the submitted contact details to review the requirement, request clarification and communicate about a potential engagement. Communication consent can be withdrawn through an official contact channel.

6. No guarantee of engagement

Nexarvia may decline or defer an enquiry when the scope, timeline, capability, legal requirements or available resources are unsuitable.
