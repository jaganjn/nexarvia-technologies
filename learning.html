Frequently asked questions about Nexarvia Learning.

Clear information before you submit the initial application.

Does application confirm enrollment?

No. Enrollment depends on eligibility, programme availability and application review.

Is a resume required?

No resume is required for the initial application. Additional documents may be requested when relevant.

Will every learner complete a live client project?

No. Project type depends on the programme, batch and genuine authorised project availability.

When is a completion credential issued?

After the learner satisfies applicable attendance, assignment, assessment and project requirements.
